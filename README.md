# Choose a guide for building AI Me

AI Me is a growing personal knowledge collection that an AI can use to help you recall decisions, develop ideas and continue your projects. Existing ChatGPT and Claude conversations provide the initial material. Bringing in other source types is a future extension.

These instructions bring together existing LLM Wiki and personal knowledge methods for beginners, with explicit choices about filtering, conversation branches, privacy, evidence and review. The contribution is synthesis and guidance, not a claim to a new technical method or a uniquely original combination.

## Choose your route

Choose one route. You do not need to read both.

| Route | What it provides | Choose it if |
|---|---|---|
| [Complete beginner guide](GUIDE.md) | Detailed explanations, desktop setup, file handling, prompts and checks gathered in one document, with links to original sources. | You want the instructions together and fewer trips between websites. Use the getting-started steps below. |
| [Shorter guide through the original sources](SOURCE_GUIDE.md) | A sequence through selected original tutorials, prompts explaining how to adapt them, and a procedure for vetting proposed downloads. | You prefer learning from the original authors with an AI helping connect their procedures. This route relies more on website access. |

Give your chosen guide's full contents to your assistant as an attachment or pasted text, rather than just its filename. Work through one stage at a time and save the instructions and progress files it helps you create. Each route explains how to continue in another chat without prior conversation history.

## Get started with the complete guide

You do not need to learn programming or understand the whole guide before beginning. Your assistant and its scripts create the needed working folders and reports as you go. If you chose the source-guided route, start with SOURCE_GUIDE.md and its opening prompt instead.

1. **Get a desktop app.** Open [section 2 of GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md#2-set-up-the-ai-assistant-and-a-folder-for-your-project) and find “Obtain and open a desktop app.” Use [DOWNLOAD_VETTING.md](DOWNLOAD_VETTING.md) with an existing assistant before obtaining a new tool, then follow the official setup links for your chosen option. App setup and exporting your chats may happen in either order.
2. **Give the assistant the instructions.** Open the app, sign in and start a new chat. Attach GUIDE.md and this README using the paperclip, plus or Add files control, or paste their full text. A filename alone does not supply the document. Attach DOWNLOAD_VETTING.md when reviewing a proposed tool.
3. **Send the message below.** Choose which provider may read your archive before attaching actual exports.

> I am a complete beginner. Help me follow the attached GUIDE.md to build a growing personal knowledge collection, starting with my old AI chats. Read the guide and this README; report any unavailable document. Follow the guide's Karpathy LLM Wiki foundation and customizations.
>
> First ask which computer and app I use and what I have already done. Help me choose a mode that can work with files and test saving in my separate working folder. Explain what you can do directly and what I must click or save myself; do not assume you can see or control my screen.
>
> Guide me from the first unfinished step. Explain what I am doing and why before each instruction. Give me one manageable action at a time and wait when you need my result. Confirm which provider may read my files. Read DOWNLOAD_VETTING.md before reviewing any new download; ask for it if unavailable. Use Python for mechanical work and the LLM for decisions. Create only the working files needed for the current stage, check a small sample before expanding, and save instructions and progress for future chats. Do not start full-archive processing, install extra tools or incur separate API charges without my approval.

For a later chat, supply your chosen guide and the latest workflow and handoff files the assistant created. They record where you stopped; you do not need a previous conversation's history.

## Included supporting documents

- [DOWNLOAD_VETTING.md](DOWNLOAD_VETTING.md) contains the review procedure and prompt to use before obtaining tools.
- [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [GOALS.md](GOALS.md) describe this documentation project's rules and mission. They govern maintaining these instructions, not the reader's personal workspace.

Keep these instructions separate from the personal workspace created while following them. The assistant and its scripts create folders and working files only when needed. For example, DOWNLOAD_VETTING.md is the supplied procedure; runs/DOWNLOAD_REVIEW.md is the report it produces in the reader’s workspace. No prefilled workspace or templates are required.

## Shared choices

Both routes preserve the original conversations and use their useful information to build connected knowledge pages. LLMs interpret meaning and make recommendations; Python handles repetitive mechanical work. You review selections and proposed pages, and citations let you check the supporting sources.

For existing chats, use only a cloud provider/account you permit to receive them. Combining ChatGPT and Claude material is a separate disclosure choice. The initial cloud-permitted route does not require installing or comparing local models. Later previously unshared material you want kept private requires a local route and tests of the actual jobs. Saving files on your computer does not mean the model runs locally.

## Scope and sources

This repository contains documentation only. It does not include an application, an archive converter, private chat exports or a prebuilt personal knowledge collection. General ingestion of other source types remains future work.

The guides link directly to the original authors and official setup instructions. They are adaptations of existing methods, including [Karpathy's LLM Wiki pattern](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f). Readers do not need an internal research bundle to follow either route.

We do not vouch for downloads offered by linked sites. Review exact tools, versions, dependencies and permissions before approving installation or execution. The included DOWNLOAD_VETTING.md supplies a reusable vetting prompt; an AI review cannot guarantee safety.

These instructions have received document reviews but have not yet been validated through an actual beginner trial. Product features, plans, menus and third-party software can change; consult current official instructions when setting up tools.
